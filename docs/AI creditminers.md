- Bank statement parsing
- Salary slip extraction

- Cash flow analysis

## C. AI Feature Engineering Pipeline

Automatically extract hundreds of ML features such as:

- Monthly income trend
- Average account balance
- Cash reserve ratio
- Spending volatility
- Salary consistency
- Seasonal income variation
- Expense categories
- Business cash flow stability
- Loan repayment behaviour
- Default indicators

---

## D. Creditworthiness Engine

Generate:

- Credit score
- Trust score
- Stability score
- Fraud score
- Liquidity score
- Income confidence score
- Behaviour score
- Overall risk grade (A–E)
- Probability of default
- Probability of late repayment

---

## E. Explainable AI (XAI)

Instead of just saying:

> **Risk Score: 72**

The system explains:

- Stable salary for 24 months (+18)
- Low debt-to-income ratio (+15)
- Excellent repayment history (+20)
- Irregular cash flow (-8)
- New employment (-6)

This makes lending decisions transparent and easier to justify.

---

## F. Recommendation Engine

AI recommends:

- Maximum safe loan amount
- Ideal interest rate
- Maximum tenure
- Required collateral (if any)
- Guarantor requirement
- Auto-approval or manual review
- Best lender matches

---

## G. Continuous Risk Monitoring

Even after a loan is approved:

- Detect income changes
- Spending behaviour changes
- Repayment risk
- Fraud indicators
- Early default prediction
- Portfolio health monitoring
